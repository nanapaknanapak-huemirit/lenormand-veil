# Card artwork attribution

The 36 card illustrations are painted scenes from a 19th-century
**Dondorf Lenormand** fortune-telling deck (B. Dondorf, Frankfurt), held by the
British Museum as object **1896,0501.308** (bequeathed by Lady Charlotte
Schreiber). The two uncut sheets were photographed by the museum and released
to the public domain.

## Sources

- [Print, playing-card (BM 1896,0501.308)](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Print,_playing-card_(BM_1896,0501.308).jpg) — sheet with 18 cards (SHA-1 `e7ae9941d347faea976ca455cf1fcaeb3f21914e`)
- [Print, playing-card (BM 1896,0501.308 1)](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Print,_playing-card_(BM_1896,0501.308_1).jpg) — sheet with 18 cards (SHA-1 `2bab6b32bf20f32a63f97db2d9e6ae4773940afb`)
- [British Museum collection record](https://www.britishmuseum.org/collection/object/P_1896-0501-308)

The paintings were cropped from the two sheets (removing the printed number
disc, playing-card inset and paper border), scaled with Lanczos resampling,
given a slight colour and contrast lift, stripped of metadata and saved as WebP.
The site's own number, playing-card inset and localized card name are rendered
in HTML/CSS. Regenerate the images with:

```bash
node scripts/fetch-card-art.mjs
```
